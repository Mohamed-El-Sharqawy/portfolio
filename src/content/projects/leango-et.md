## The brief

An enterprise social network inside the LeanGo suite. Employees build profiles, track how their skills grow, and connect across the organization's training ecosystem.

## My part

I contributed to the E&T frontend as part of the team building the LeanGo platform.

## How it was built

- **Next.js 15 + React 19** with **TypeScript**.
- **React Query** for server state, **Tailwind CSS** for the UI.

## Outcome

Skills become visible across the whole organization, not just inside individual teams.
