## The brief

Global Lean Industry (gLi) needed three enterprise products — facility inspection, warehouse management, and an employee skills network — with granular role-based permissions and reporting for enterprise operations.

## My part

I helped build the full suite, translating Figma wireframes and mockups into interactive prototypes and pixel-accurate production interfaces, iterating daily with product designers from design through release. All three products shipped from **one shared React/Next.js codebase** with reusable, typed component patterns.

## The three systems

- **Inspection** — facility-management workflows: scheduling, site walkthroughs, defect capture, and reporting across large property portfolios.
- **WMS** — warehouse management views covering inventory and stock movement, with role-based access and operations reporting.
- **E&T** — an enterprise social network where employees build profiles and track skill growth across the organization's training ecosystem.

## How it was built

- **React 19 + Next.js 15** with **TypeScript** end to end.
- **React Query** for server state; **Tailwind CSS** for the shared design language.
- Shared, typed component patterns so each system reused the same permissions and reporting primitives.

## Outcome

Three production systems, one codebase, one design system — new product views came from composing existing primitives instead of rebuilding them.
