## The brief

A case-management platform for crime and legal records, part of the national-scale systems Global Dynamics delivers in Iraq. The audience is large (1M+ potential users) and the data is sensitive, so three requirements shaped every screen: **strict access control**, **auditability**, and **heavy concurrent use**.

## My part

I worked full-stack on the platform: the **case-management interfaces** and the access-aware views that only show what a role may see, together with **database design, data handling and system architecture** decisions shared with the team — the patterns that keep record history traceable and every action attributable.

## How it was built

- **Next.js 16 + React 19** on the frontend, typed end to end with **TypeScript**.
- **NestJS** services behind it, shipped in **Docker**.
- Performance work shared across the Global Dynamics systems: code-splitting, caching and memoization, so heavy screens stay responsive under concurrent load.

## Outcome

One platform built to serve a national user base, with security and traceability treated as product features rather than afterthoughts.
