## The brief

A gifting and flower e-commerce store where the business controls everything — catalog and content — from a headless CMS, connected to their CRM.

## My part

I built the platform at Forever Events: the storefront, the **CMS-driven** catalog and content, the **CRM integration**, and the **SEO** work. It drew on the reusable, accessible component library I authored and shared across Forever Events projects.

## How it was built

- **Next.js 15 + React 19** with **TypeScript** and **Tailwind CSS**.
- **Headless CMS** for catalog and content.
- Technical SEO as part of the build, not an afterthought.

## Outcome

Top rankings for the client's core business keywords.
