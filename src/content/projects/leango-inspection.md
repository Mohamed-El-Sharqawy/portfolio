## The brief

Facility-management teams inspect large property portfolios. LeanGo Inspection turns that into a digital workflow: **scheduling**, **site walkthroughs**, **defect capture** and **reporting**.

## My part

I built the facility-management inspection workflows in the LeanGo suite.

## How it was built

- **Next.js 15 + React 19** with **TypeScript**.
- **React Query** for server state, **Tailwind CSS** for the UI.

## Outcome

Inspection operations unified across the whole facility portfolio.
