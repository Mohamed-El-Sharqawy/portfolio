## The brief

Part of Grwan Group's LeanGo platform suite for enterprise operations. The WMS covers inventory, stock movement and operations reporting, used by both warehouse staff and management.

## My part

I built the warehouse-management views, with **role-based access** so warehouse and management users each get the screens and reports they need, and the **reporting** interfaces on top of operations data.

## How it was built

- **Next.js 15 + React 19** with **TypeScript**.
- **React Query** for server state, **Tailwind CSS** for the UI.

## Outcome

Warehouse operations running on one platform instead of scattered tools.
